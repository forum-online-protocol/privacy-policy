# Forum Online Safety Policy

**App Name:** Forum Online  
**Developer:** Forum Online  
**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

Forum Online contains user-generated content, including posts, proposals, comments, profile text, and links shared by users. We are committed to maintaining a safe environment while allowing lawful civic discussion and debate.

## Child Safety Standards

Forum Online strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to all users and all content on Forum Online, including text, images, videos, links, posts, proposals, comments, and profiles.

A child is anyone under 18. Prohibited conduct includes creating, uploading, requesting, sharing, promoting, or linking to CSAM; grooming; sexualizing minors; sexual extortion involving a child; and facilitating the sexual exploitation or trafficking of children. Real, altered, and computer-generated depictions of child sexual abuse are prohibited.

These rules apply regardless of a user's age, account status, or identity verification.

## Zero-Tolerance Categories

The following are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate removal, account suspension, permanent banning, and reporting to relevant authorities where appropriate:

- Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including CSAM, grooming, sexualization of minors, or any child exploitation content
- Credible threats of violence, violent incitement, or terrorism-related promotion
- Content that facilitates serious illegal activity
- Non-consensual sexual content or sexual extortion
- Doxxing, exposure of private personal data, or targeted harassment

## Other Prohibited Content

Forum Online does not allow:

- Hate speech or degrading attacks against protected groups
- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, or stalking
- Spam, scams, phishing, impersonation, or coordinated manipulation
- Pornographic sexual content or sexual services promotion
- Graphic gore posted to shock or abuse other users
- Malware, harmful links, or attempts to compromise accounts or devices
- Copyright or trademark infringement

## Reporting And Review

### Report Within The App

Users can submit concerns about posts and comments directly inside Forum Online, without opening a browser or email application. In-app reporting is available to signed-in users and guests. Guests do not need to create an account, connect a wallet, or complete identity verification to report publicly visible content.

To report a post:

1. Find the post in the feed or open its detail screen.
2. Open the post's three-dot menu and select **Report Post**.
3. Select a reason, including **Child safety concerns** for suspected child abuse or exploitation.
4. Optionally add a short description and select **Send report**.

To report a comment, open the post's comments and select **Report** on the comment, then choose a reason and submit. Labels may be translated into the app's selected language.

A successful submission displays a confirmation and saves the report for moderator review. If submission fails, the app displays an error; retry rather than assuming the report was received. Submitting a report does not automatically remove the reported content.

### Other Safety Concerns And Contact

For concerns that are not tied to a reportable post or comment, or if in-app reporting is unavailable, contact **mail@forum.online** for child safety concerns or **safety@forum.online** for general safety reports. Email is an additional channel, not a requirement for using the in-app report form.

Include the post or comment reference, a link if available, and a brief explanation. Do not download, copy, attach, or redistribute suspected CSAM to make a report. Do not include identity documents or other unnecessary personal information.

### Review And Response

Reports are placed in a moderation queue. Reports submitted under **Child safety concerns** are marked urgent for priority review. We aim to review safety concerns and take appropriate action promptly; response times depend on the risk and the information available, and no fixed response time is guaranteed.

If a child may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority. Forum Online's reporting channels are not emergency services.

## Handling Suspected Or Confirmed CSAM

Our moderation procedure requires reviewers to assess child safety reports, restrict access to harmful content where appropriate, and remove or disable access to CSAM when we become aware of it. Related accounts may be restricted or suspended under the enforcement rules below.

Confirmed CSAM must be escalated to the relevant regional authority or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as applicable. Reporting, preservation of relevant records, and responses to lawful requests must follow applicable child safety laws. These steps are handled by the responsible safety contact; users must not collect or redistribute abusive material themselves.

## Enforcement Actions

Depending on severity, context, and repeat behavior, Forum Online may:

- Reject content before publication
- Remove published content
- Disable comments or interactions on a post
- Restrict account capabilities
- Suspend or permanently ban accounts
- Block abusive users from continued use of the service
- Escalate to law enforcement or child-protection organizations when required

Verified identity or passport-based access does not exempt any user from moderation or enforcement.

## Community Standards

Forum Online is built for civic and governance discussion. Controversial opinions, criticism, and political disagreement are not removed simply for being unpopular. Moderation focuses on safety, legality, abuse prevention, privacy protection, and platform integrity.
